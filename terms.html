What Mandhari is

Mandhari helps you prepare, pay for and track government service requests. It is independent and is not a government service.

Fees

Government fees are paid by you directly to the agency. Mandhari charges a separate convenience fee, shown before you pay.

Your responsibilities

  • Give accurate information and use only your own identity and accounts.
  • Keep your PIN and device safe.
  • Do not misuse the service or try to access other people’s data.

Availability and accuracy

Mandhari depends on government systems we do not control. We try to keep results accurate and the service available, but we cannot guarantee either.
