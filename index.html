Common questions

Is Mandhari a government app?

No. Mandhari is an independent app that helps you use official government services.

Can you see my government password?

No. You log in to the official portal yourself, and we never see or store those credentials.

What does it cost?

You pay the government fee directly to the agency. Mandhari adds a small convenience fee, shown before you pay.
