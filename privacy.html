Privacy policy

Last updated: [date]

Who we are

Mandhari is an independent app that helps you use Kenyan government services. We are not run by the Government of Kenya. Contact: hello@mandhari.example.

What we collect

  • Your WhatsApp number or email, to sign you in.
  • The result of your one-time identity check.
  • Your requests: the service, reference numbers, status and receipts you choose to keep.
  • Basic device and usage information to keep the service secure and working.

What we never collect

Your passwords for government portals, your M-Pesa PIN, or your card details.

How we use it

To run the service you ask for, to prevent fraud, to send you status updates, and to improve Mandhari.

Who we share it with

Government agencies, only to complete a request you start. Service providers that help us run Mandhari (hosting, messaging, payments, identity checks), under written agreements. We do not sell your data.

How long we keep it

[Add retention periods.] You can ask us to delete your account and data.

Your rights

Under the Data Protection Act, 2019 you can ask to access, correct or delete your data, and to object to certain uses. See our data protection page.
